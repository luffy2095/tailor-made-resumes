# Track: hospitality revenue management

Revenue analysts and managers, cluster and regional revenue roles, distribution and channel managers, and adjacent hotel commercial roles.

## Defaults
- Human layout: two-column sidebar. Photo: on is customary in India, the Middle East and much of Asia; off for the US and UK. Ask.
- Section order: Profile, Experience, Achievements; sidebar holds contact, skills, systems, education, professional development.
- Employer numbers are often confidential. Default to qualitative language unless the user says otherwise.

## Proof to ask for in discovery
- Which hotels, how many keys, which segments (transient, corporate, group, MICE, wedding).
- Which systems at which depth: revenue management system, property management system, channel manager, rate shopper, benchmarking reports.
- Pricing decisions owned versus recommended. Forecast horizon and who it went to.
- Group evaluations and displacement decisions made.

## Keyword bank
RevPAR, ADR, occupancy, GOPPAR, TRevPAR, forecasting, pickup, pace, on-the-books, displacement analysis, group evaluation, BAR, length-of-stay restrictions, overbooking, channel mix, OTA management, distribution cost, rate parity, market segmentation, competitive set, STR benchmarking (MPI, ARI, RGI), budget and reforecast, total revenue management.

Common systems (claim only those in the dossier): IDeaS, Duetto, Oracle OPERA, rate shoppers, channel managers, STR reports, Excel and Power Query, SQL, Power BI or Tableau.

## Cheat sheet focus
- **Core metrics through one night at the candidate's hotel**: occupancy, ADR and RevPAR from one day's arrivals, and why RevPAR = occupancy × ADR.
- **Rituals**: the daily pickup review, the weekly revenue meeting, the monthly STR review.
- **Worked story**: a group displacement decision with invented numbers: the group's total value against the transient revenue it displaces.
- **Vocabulary**: the target brand's own names for its systems and reports, to recognise, not claim.
- **TRAPs**: averaging ADR across days instead of total revenue over total room nights, comparing occupancy without the same room count, booking date versus stay date, RGI below 100 meaning losing share even when RevPAR grew.

## Common LEARN gaps and topic sheets
- SQL for revenue analysts, with bookings and hotels tables.
- Power Query or Tableau for revenue reporting.
- STR benchmarking and indexes.
- Finance and MIS: P&L flow-through, GOP, budgeting.
- Moving to OTAs or vacation rentals: supply-side metrics and account management.
